Vietnamese Localization Extension:
- Generated deterministically by scripts/build_hint_vn.py from scripts/Hint_base.ttf.
- Preserves all 322 original m5x7 glyphs and line metrics (hhea ascent=682, descent=-128, lineGap=92) unchanged.
- Adds 94 precomposed NFC Vietnamese letters (completing all 134/134 Vietnamese alphabet forms) and 6 UI punctuation/currency symbols (• – — … → ₫).
